- [ ] Replace the public prefilled-issue handoff with an anonymous in-game report endpoint backed by a narrowly scoped GitHub App. Issues should be authored by a dedicated NanoSwarm feedback bot, keep credentials entirely server-side, use validation, rate limiting and abuse protection, and clearly label reports as anonymous player submissions.

- [x] Stage 2 chassis boundary and delayed atmospheric discovery.
- [x] Scale outward material-search cohorts and durations with each local mass horizon.
- [x] Keep nested-substrate survey controls and progress in the substrate panel rather than the recurring cohort board.
- [x] Event-led catalogue research split into Residuum Indexing, chassis iron analysis and atmospheric spectroscopy without overloading the first material horizons.
- [x] Apply Ferromagnetic Phase Analysis to re-sort retained iron into usable elemental inventory.
- [x] Keep captured atmosphere physically separate from solid Feedstock and Residuum.

- [ ] Add aggregate early presentation, source-lot controls and later Spectral Binning without homogenizing compositions.
- [x] Remove generated refinement tiers from the playable research catalogue.
- [ ] Re-author later morphology research without changing the shared nanite recipe.
- [ ] Author physical specialist assembly, populations, recipes and role-specific construction times for later morphology tiers.
- [ ] Phase-aware bulk collection, recovery, tailings and specialist morphology efficiencies.
- [ ] Authored soil, water and flora fields following the chassis boundary.
- [ ] Introduce free-ranging biomass search or hunting parties as operational directives at local-area scale.

## Chemistry and morphology

- [ ] Separate element identification, compound recognition and compound decomposition.
- [ ] Progress through polymer, oxide, aqueous and biological processing into universal molecular disassembly and assembly.
- [ ] Keep flux-based nuclear transmutation post-human, infrastructure-heavy and extremely expensive.
- [ ] Treat Morphologies II+ as interchangeable tools and temporary equipment on the canonical nanite body.

## Energy, storage and thermal systems

See [`energy-locality-storage-and-thermal-model.md`](energy-locality-storage-and-thermal-model.md).

- [ ] Resolve the player-facing 20 pJ versus authored 40 pJ energy-job discrepancy and lock the result with runtime/UI tests.
- [ ] Add derived power diagnostics that distinguish collector processing ceiling from realised local power.
- [ ] Preserve linear microscopic energy yield below environmental capacity while saturating additional workers above it.
- [ ] Author passive-gradient energy sources by substrate shell, including source power, accessible interface and any finite free-energy reserve.
- [ ] Guarantee a progression-safe minimum trickle and enough start-up energy to construct the first alternative source.
- [ ] Add explicit stored-energy capacity, maximum charge power and maximum discharge power.
- [ ] Ensure a full store throttles, spills or redirects production through an explicit deterministic rule rather than deleting energy silently.
- [ ] Add at least one persistent Stage 2 electrical or electrochemical power installation using ordinary matter and atmospheric or scavenged inputs.
- [ ] Track conversion losses as heat and introduce a first local thermal-capacity and heat-rejection abstraction.
- [ ] Make large power use, direct electrical theft and ablation contribute heat, electromagnetic, acoustic and chemical signatures.
- [ ] Require Directed Bond Ablation to reserve its full coupling charge, satisfy discharge-power limits and emit an authored thermal event.
- [ ] Keep vacuum or zero-point extraction outside early and mid-game progression; any later exotic source still requires finite coupling density and entropy disposal.

## Growth pacing and directive coherence

- [x] Gate replication-efficiency diagnostics and Temporary Burst behind a 180-nanite Cohort Ratio Prognostics signal.
- [x] Compare live and coherent geometric substrate-conversion ETAs so ratio changes communicate their real time multiplier.
- [x] Forecast active-substrate exhaustion from the current collection share and replication growth curve.
- [x] Batch partial replication inputs for five seconds while upstream payloads converge, limiting phase fragmentation.
- [x] Give Temporary Burst a replication-hold charging state so a coherent pipeline can actually build its minimum buffer.
- [x] Report the exact number of assigned replicators unable to reserve a complete recipe.
- [x] Distinguish a genuine missing-input halt from waiting on enough already-scheduled upstream payloads.
- [x] Score Collect, Sort, Energy and Replicate allocations against the exact sustainable pipeline ratio.
- [x] Expose the complete-recipe buffer and its limiting identified input.
- [x] Require 30 continuous seconds at 99% replication efficiency before burst control is eligible.
- [x] Implement Temporary Burst with exact whole-recipe reservation, refundable undispatched inputs, and restoration of prior targets and locks.
- [ ] Test a later τ-coherence acceleration layer after the allocation loop has real play data.

## Entropy and humanity

- [ ] Add damaged, immobilized, captured, dispersed and destroyed nanite states with matter-conserving recovery.
- [ ] Introduce environmental attrition during Stage 2 and deliberate human countermeasures during Stage 4.
- [ ] Drive human detection through heat, emissions, grid use, visible conversion, escaped nanites and player strategy.
- [ ] Author escalation from anomaly investigation through containment, military resistance and Grey Goo planetary conflict.
